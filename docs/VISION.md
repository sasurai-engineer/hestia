# VISION

This is the governing document. Every increment — surface, pack, engine, mockup,
ticket — is judged against it, the way code is judged against the tests. It was not
asserted; it was investigated: five research dives (the competitive field, the
masters of interface craft, the owner's actual year, the unbound possibilities of
the substrate, Texas primary law), pressure-tested by an adversarial refutation
pass, closed into a synthesis brief, and authored here. The provenance appendix at
the bottom maps every load-bearing claim to its source, because a vision document
for this product that could not show its work would fail its own first pillar.

**Correct is the entry fee, not the deliverable.** That sentence is the reason this
document exists, and nobody should ever have to say it to this project again.

---

## 1. What Hestia is

Hestia is named for the goddess whose fire is kept, not brandished, and the product
must deserve the name.

In a field of seventeen serious products, sixteen are systems of record — type,
store, chart — descended from ancestors that never held the owner's actual jobs:
the tax bill, the appeal, the notice that must quote the statute or be dismissed,
the hold-or-sell call. Hestia is the first **system of judgment**. It is built on a
substrate without peer — law as cited, effective-dated, correctable data;
provenance on every fact; twin engines proven to the cent under mutation — and it
renders finished verdicts at the moments of the owner's year, in two forms with one
grammar: a pocket verdict machine for the moments that find you standing up, and a
seasonal desk instrument for the weeks when the filings come in lattices.

It does not yet fly the plane and does not claim to; it is a flight director
earning the word autopilot one servo at a time. Its default screen is not a queue
of your obligations but the proof that you have none, and every number on that
screen can show you the county page it came from. Its work product is drafted
before it is asked for; its answers survive their second click; its silence is a
deliverable, not a gap in one.

**In one sentence:** Hestia is the first system of judgment for the people who own
the building — it derives every date from cited law, arrives with the argument
already drafted, proves every number to its source page, and measures its mastery
in the owners' uninterrupted nights.

## 2. The pillars

### P1 — Every number answers

Every number Hestia shows can decompose, on demand, to the ledger entry, the
statute section, or the county page it came from — and a number that cannot is a
compile error, not a style violation. The DecisionCard grammar — **verdict +
authority + one action** — is the atom of every surface on every device. The nine
tables are demoted to the drill's landing floors: evidence, never navigation.

This is the pillar the refutation pass could not dent from any angle, which is why
it is first. It answers the absolution problem (trust is minted in the two drills a
year that matter), the cloning problem (a competitor's demo dies at its second
click, because their data's origins were discarded at entry, years ago), and the
liability problem (inspectability is the defense).

### P2 — The advisor is the interface

The AI advisor is not a pane in the product; it is the product wearing three faces:

- **the turn** is the advisor's agenda,
- **the drafted filing** is the advisor's work product,
- **the provenance drill** is the advisor under cross-examination.

The three faces ship in act one. Chat is a fourth, subordinate door —
palette-invoked, answering only in DecisionCards over tool-shaped APIs, where
verdicts, authorities, and typed gaps make a hallucinated number structurally
impossible. Any mockup that leads with a chat box dies on sight; a chat box is the
incumbents' move.

### P3 — Silence is the deliverable

For most of the year Hestia is a correspondent, not a cockpit. The honest
arithmetic: roughly 60–100 real sessions a year across two operators, monthly in
the dead months. So the product is designed for episodic, high-stakes visits —
recognition over recall; state that re-introduces itself after absence ("since
June: Hamilton County moved its dates twice — here is the diff; Bri handled the
Nashville renewal"); quiet dark as the native register; juice only as a settle on
covenant-kept moments. The home state is proof that nothing needs you, and the
notification channel is a primary surface, not a delivery seam.

Dropped outright: ambient across-the-room displays, daily-ritual habituation, any
UI whose payoff requires a mastery curve monthly visits cannot accrue. Three
urgency classes — **interrupt-now / next-session / on-record** — are standing law,
so that dates which err early never cry wolf.

### P4 — Two souls, one grammar

Hestia is two instruments sharing one DecisionCard atom:

- **The pocket verdict machine** — one card, one verdict, one action; dark room,
  huge type. The heir and generalization of the already-built 11pm dispatch
  overlay, which was the first surface with a soul and is the seed of all of this.
- **The seasonal desk instrument** — density, lenses, the scrubbable time axis,
  drag-an-assumption recompute, for the six-odd weeks a year of filing seasons.

The moments disagree about the device — fear-ranked moments skew phone,
dollar-dense moments skew desk — and a responsive median of the two is exactly what
incumbents ship, so the median is refused. Time-as-a-place and lenses are desk-only
claims. The phone never shows a dashboard; the desk never buzzes.

### P5 — Two operators, one record; ingest is ratification

Nothing enters Hestia without a source and nothing issues from it without a name.
Documents arrive by photograph, forward, or drop; they are extracted, proposed, and
**ratified** — the owner corrects, never types into a blank. Every judgment that
leaves records who ratified it, when, and what the system showed them at that
moment — which the bitemporal read model already makes possible and which, like all
provenance, cannot be retrofitted.

Every surface carries an owner-of-record, a handoff gesture, and whose-week-it-is,
because a couple-run LLC must have no single point of failure at 2am or on
April 15. v1 deliberately omits roles, permissions, and approval chains: two people
who trust each other need a record, not a workflow engine.

### P6 — Earned, not asserted

Hestia says only what it can prove today. It is a flight director with a servo
roadmap; its covenant warrants the derivation, not the world; its moat is the
**compiler** — pack schema, gap typing, supersession, differential fixtures, the
prediction-scored research loop the Texas pack demonstrated — never the coverage.

Each of the twelve moments names its actuator (certified-mail API, payment rail,
e-file or filing-agent partnership, arbitration escrow) and the date its yoke
stows. The word "autopilot" is unspeakable until one moment runs end-to-end with
zero owner action. The outcomes warranty waits for the actuarial run on the
founders' own four properties. A jurisdiction is added only when a door is, and
authority #21 must cost less to bank than authority #1 — a tracked, first-class
metric; until that ratio falls, the moat is defensible craft.

v1's named buyer is the operator-founder himself. Founder delight is forbidden as
evidence for the claims v1 structurally cannot test: absolution demand, strangers'
session frequency, willingness to pay. This pillar is the honesty budget the other
five spend from.

## 3. The form

### The geometry

Every Hestia screen is a position along three axes:

- **time** — when: ledger behind, statutory calendar ahead;
- **place** — which property;
- **depth** — how proven: verdict → derivation → source document.

The turn is a position (now, everywhere, verdict-level). The drill is motion along
depth. A lens is one question asked across all places at fixed depth. The timeline
is motion along time. **There is no noun navigation.** The nine tables are the
deepest floor of the depth axis, reached only by drilling; no table is a
destination in any nav. This geometry is the never-seen-before claim, stated so
mockups can be judged against it.

### What the owner sees on open — phone, which is most days

**The Hearth.** Four dark property rows. A covenant strip naming the next statutory
date with its authority, freshness stamp, and monitor status ("Newport city tax —
Oct 31 — KRS-cited — verified Aug 30 — watch live"). The ledger of silence accruing
beneath: windows met, dollars defended, corrections received — counted from day
one. When a decision genuinely blocks, the Hearth becomes the turn: at most three
cards, verdict first, one action each, drill on tap, owner-of-record on every card.
After more than three weeks away, the Hearth re-introduces its state before it asks
for anything.

The design spec, verbatim, is the sentence the whole investigation stands behind:
*Hestia's default screen is not a queue of your obligations; it is the proof that
you have none — and every number on it can show you the county page it came from.*

### What the owner sees on open — desk, in season

The same world in season mode: the time axis across the top, scrubbable; the
portfolio table wearing lenses (cashflow / deadline / over-assessment / hold-sell);
the turn queue at right; the command palette holding the verbs and the "ask" door.
Drag-an-assumption recompute lives here and only here — the hold/sell fan, the
December prepay lever, the renewal EV — because watching the judgment re-derive is
how the engine teaches its reasoning.

### The first hour

Onboarding, the demo, and the pre-purchase dossier are one mechanism. Four
addresses in; law resolves per parcel while you watch (Newport's one-street-over
URLTA line drawn live); the first turn assembles itself ("three things need
deciding before this week closes — one is a Texas PIR you didn't know existed,
personal liability attached"); the first drill (tap the $4,100, land on the county
page); the first drafted output awaiting dissent. The same hour run on a candidate
address is the underwriting dossier for property #5. Any mockup that cannot be
placed somewhere in this hour is decoration.

### The good days

The earned-empty turn is a designed state, not an absence. The covenant ledger
accrues in view. Each year closes with the **Annual Report to Ourselves** —
engine-generated, provenance-backed, set in accountant typography: what the
portfolio earned, what the system defended, what the forecast fan now says. It is
the dossier's sibling, pointed at the owners' own pride, and it is what makes year
two feel like an instrument.

### The first four mockups

Chosen for v1's real life — the next five months are bill season, the discount
window, the prepay fork, and the January check:

1. **The Hearth** — one mockup, both souls: the phone default and the desk default
   of the same surface. The identity claim rendered; the first thing review settles.
2. **The October Turn** — the desk instrument in season mode on the real portfolio:
   TN and TX bills landing; Newport's two-bills-one-house plus the Oct 15 rental
   license and Oct 31 city tax; the Dallas registration anniversary; the November
   KY 2% discount pre-staged as free money nobody notices — with the drill
   demonstrated on the Texas bill's number down to the county page.
3. **The January Check** — the covenant card at full ceremony for the scariest
   scheduled moment the founders will actually live: the ~$12–20K January 31
   payment; the February 1 cliff (6% + 1% automatic, 20% attorney add-on July 1);
   the December prepay fork it inherits; owner-of-record and handoff; and the yoke
   rendered honestly — "Hestia has prepared everything; you move the money," or the
   first servo, per open question 3.
4. **The Standing Appeal** — the desk's full ceremony, due before Act III opens.

The 11pm dispatch overlay stands as pocket mockup zero.

## 4. The refusals

Each is load-bearing; together they are the opinion.

1. **No invented number.** A gap is an answer, typed and rendered; a blank is a
   defect; an ungrounded DecisionCard does not compile.
2. **No arithmetic the law does not authorize.** No summed rates, no ratio applied
   across a value_basis boundary — the 35%-ratio bug is the clone's signature,
   never ours.
3. **No threshold nobody published.** Every boundary cites its authority and its
   effective dates — the ≤4-unit line, the de-minimis line, the franchise line,
   all of them.
4. **No anchoring a statutory window to notice arrival.** Conditional notices
   (TX s.25.19, KY's envelope that never comes) anchor to the unconditional
   statutory date, labeled.
5. **No chat-box advisor.** Chat is a door, never the product; a mockup leading
   with chat is rejected on sight.
6. **No navigation by table.** Nouns are evidence at the bottom of the drill, not
   destinations in a nav bar.
7. **No tolling the owner's cash flow.** Money movement is evidence, never
   territory; revenue is never a percentage of the owner's own rent.
8. **No settings page standing in for a decision the product should have made.**
   One correct way per workflow.
9. **No speculative jurisdiction, ever.** Depth-per-address, never
   breadth-per-map: a state is added when a door is, and each new address pays for
   its own research.
10. **No blank fields.** The owner ratifies and corrects; the owner never types a
    number the system should have extracted.
11. **No dashboard on the phone, no buzzing at the desk.** Each soul keeps its
    register.
12. **No unearned words.** "Autopilot" waits for the first stowed yoke; "warranty"
    extends no further than the derivation proves; "never" is spoken only about
    what the type system enforces.
13. **No crying wolf.** Interrupt-now is reserved for its urgency class; the first
    false alarm is treated as a covenant breach of its own kind.
14. **No founder delight as market evidence.** v1 proves derivation correctness,
    cost-per-authority, and penalty-free time; it proves nothing about strangers,
    and this document says so.

## 5. The covenant

The honest form of the promise, for a four-state v1, in the product's own voice:

> **Every date Hestia shows derives from a cited, effective-dated authority, and
> you can watch it derive.** Unconditional statutory dates anchor to the statute;
> conditional and per-parcel legs anchor to an unconditional date and are labeled
> for what they are. Every source wears a freshness stamp and a monitor status you
> can see — when we last verified it against the world, and whether the watch is
> live. Where the law is silent, moved, or unverified, Hestia shows a typed gap:
> absence is an answer here, never a blank. When the world moves — a board
> adjourns early, a county extends, a session amends — the correction finds you as
> a diff, delivered in the same urgency discipline as the original: statutory
> anchors interrupt; watched instances inform. **If a date on this screen does not
> follow from its cited authority as of its stamp, that is our defect, and the
> record will show it.** What Hestia does not yet promise is to pay for an
> outcome. That warranty is being underwritten the only honest way there is: on
> our own four properties, in a ledger that has been counting since day one —
> windows met, dollars defended, corrections received — penalty-free year by
> penalty-free year.

Two epistemic grades, never blurred: the **derivation** (warranted now) and the
**world** (watched, stamped, and corrected — warranted only as far as the stamp).
The covenant ledger is instrumented from Act I day one, because "zero missed
windows across N statutory deadlines since 2026" can only be said later if counted
now. v1's obligation to user #2 is instrumentation, not persuasion.

## 6. The bar

The acceptance test for every increment. An agent self-applies it before calling
anything done. A "no" on any line is a finding; findings rank rather than veto only
at mockup review, where the founder decides.

1. **The entry fee.** Every number cites its authority; every gap is typed;
   fixtures hold to the cent; mutation gates pass; an invented number fails the
   build. Correct is the entry fee, not the deliverable.
2. **The second click.** Does every number survive its second click — drill to
   derivation, derivation to source? Could a clone render this screen? If yes at
   depth zero, it must die at depth one.
3. **The verdict test.** Does the surface lead with the finished judgment and
   reserve interaction for inspection and dissent?
4. **The silence test.** Is a compliant property dark? Does anything here speak
   without an urgency class?
5. **The soul test.** Is this pocket or desk, and does it keep its register — no
   dashboard in the pocket, no buzz at the desk?
6. **The two-operator test.** Owner-of-record, handoff, whose-week-it-is —
   present?
7. **The moment test.** Which of the twelve moments does this serve, and where
   does it sit in the first hour? An increment serving no moment is decoration.
8. **The word test.** Does any copy claim what P6 has not yet earned — autopilot,
   warranty, never?
9. **The cost-curve test** (pack work only). Did this authority cost less to bank
   than the last, and was the ratio recorded?
10. **The founder test.** Would this stop a 40-year architect mid-scroll — is
    there one thing on this surface he has never seen anywhere, and is that thing
    true? Astonishment without provenance fails; provenance without astonishment
    is the incumbents with citations.

## 7. The sequence

Three acts for two people and seven doors, each named for the moment it serves,
each shipping the advisor's faces — never deferring them. The chat door alone
waits, because chat inherits credibility and cannot create it.

**Act I — "The Bills Arrive"** (now → January 31). Serves bill season and its
cliffs, the November KY 2% discount, the December prepay lever, the January Check;
standing coverage for the 2am moment (already built) and the drawer letter. Ships:
the Hearth in both souls; the DecisionCard grammar and the drill live on real
October bills; the correspondent channel as a primary surface; the covenant ledger
counting from day one; the Texas pack wired from the banked research. Board
tickets: the Texas pack (#129); the wrong-money lane (#102–#104 — money classes
must be right before a single bill renders); #38 delivery, promoted from seam to
primary surface; #40 auth — two named operators are the prerequisite of the
two-operator record.

**Act II — "The April Lattice"** (February → April 15). Serves year-end close, the
1065/K-1 deadline, the nine-to-ten-filing April pileup — FONCE renewal on its
exactly-four-units knife edge, LLET, TN annual report, Newport occupational
license, Cincinnati 1.8% — the capex fork at PO time, the 1099-NEC under its new
threshold. Ships: the desk instrument's season mode in earnest (density, lenses,
the time axis); ingest-as-ratification at volume; the Annual Report to Ourselves;
dual-book depreciation surfaces with CONFIRM-WITH-CPA calibration.

**Act III — "The Protest Ritual"** (April 15 → July). Serves the KY 13-day window
designed to be missed (PVA conference prerequisite, one-workday appeal), the
May 15 Texas protest with evidence and opinion at filing (income approach for the
four-plex, circuit-breaker verified against its 2026-12-31 sunset), the PIR that
carries personal liability, the TN board ladder climbed in order, the ARB-order
arbitration fork with its deposit. Ships: the Standing Appeal as the desk's full
ceremony — the most visceral demo of law-as-data; the advisor harness as the
palette's ask door (the fourth face, arriving after the three faces have earned
it); the first servo per the founder's answer to open question 3 — at minimum,
#38 matured into certified-mail delivery, the covenant's one admitted seam closed
with a receipt.

Metrics carried across all acts, because they are the only claims v1 can prove:
**derivation correctness** (fixtures and mutation), **cost-per-authority** (the
compiler curve), **penalty-free time** (the ledger).

## 8. The open register

Registered, not settled — the vision must not pretend otherwise. Mirrors the p0
register in ARCHITECTURE.md.

- **The UPL line.** Per-state unauthorized-practice-of-law boundaries for drafted
  filings and notices — moot for v1 on the founders' own properties, decisive for
  what v2 may say out loud.
- **Pricing posture.** Outcome-aligned versus flat, and the
  contingency-displacement collision with the appeal-services industry.
- **Pack maintenance at scale.** Hamilton County's twice-moved dates are the
  standing exhibit; the cost-per-authority curve is the instrument that watches
  this.

## 9. Open questions for the founder

Three genuine forks; everything else in this document is decided.

1. **Mockup convergence.** Two design tracks exist — this document and the
   sibling agent's mockups. Do both now converge through the bar (§6) at one
   review, with tests ranking rather than vetoing — or is the mockup set a
   competing vision to be settled first? One process must be named before either
   track cuts more mockups.
2. **The public actuarial run.** The outcomes warranty can only be underwritten by
   the covenant ledger on your own four properties — real addresses, real
   dollars, real receipts. Public from the start, or private-with-receipts until
   v2? It is your family's data, so it is your call alone.
3. **The first money servo.** January 31 is the one moment where "autopilot" could
   be earned inside v1 — Hestia moving your own ~$12–20K to the county,
   end-to-end, zero owner action, with the two of you as the only users. Build
   that single payment rail this winter, or does v1 stay a pure flight director,
   certified mail its only actuator, the money always in your hand? The answer
   sets the servo roadmap's first entry and whether the January Check mockup
   shows a button or a proof.
