### What the Engines phase delivered

Eight modules, every figure exact, every failure a `DomainError`:

- **Amortization** — level-payment schedules, HalfUp per the lender convention,
  final-payment plug; the $300k/6.75%/360 note reproduces its lender schedule
  to the cent ($1,945.79; total interest $400,489.69).
- **MACRS depreciation** — SL mid-month, 200/150DB half-year and mid-quarter,
  computed from the statutory formulas; the one exactly-exact Pub 946 column
  (5-year half-year) is asserted digit for digit. §179 → bonus → MACRS
  ordering; **dual-book**: the same asset at 100% federal bonus and under
  Kentucky's 31 Dec 2001 conformity (no bonus, §179 capped by
  `kentuckySection179Limit` at $25k less the over-$200k phaseout).
- **Disposal** — §1245 ordinary recapture vs unrecaptured §1250, the split that
  prices a cost-seg *through the exit*; the plan's worked example ($29,500 at
  25% + $12,000 capital = $41,500) is a fixture.
- **NPV/IRR** — exact discounting, deterministic bisection with a bounded
  iteration budget and honest bracket errors.
- **Rent renewal EV** — 12·Δ·P(stay) − P(leave)·(turn + vacancy), with the
  tie-to-smaller-increase recommendation rule.
- **Coinsurance & loss-of-rents** — the penalty factor named, the recovery
  capped and floored, the rebuild-gap priced.
- **Hold/sell** — forward-year total return over current equity (cash flow +
  paydown + appreciation + tax shield), net-of-friction sale proceeds, verdict
  against the redeployment hurdle.

**The differential contract:** every fixture in
`packages/engines/fixtures/engine-fixtures.json` was generated by an
independent Python implementation (`services/sim/hestia_sim/finance.py` —
`fractions`/`decimal`, not decimal.js) and both languages must reproduce it to
the cent in CI. Two implementations, one expectation set, zero tolerance.

## The p0 register — what the product is wrong or liable without

Ordered for execution; struck items are closed and kept for the record.

1. ~~Recapture modelled through exit~~ — **closed**: `disposalAnalysis` +
   `disposalTax`, fixture-proven.
2. ~~Capex survival mathematics~~ — **closed**: `hestia_sim.capex`, seeded
   PCG64, conditional-Weibull first failures, percentile bands.
3. ~~Deadline calendar~~ — **closed (spine + engine)**: the `deadlines` table
   (anchored, cited, statused) and `deadlines.ts` — the KY inspection window
   (KRS 133.045, 13 days excluding Sundays; 2026's May 4-18 is the verified
   anchor, 2027's May 3-17 is the first live test), §6654(c) estimated-tax
   dates, 1099-NEC, §1031 clocks, reminder schedules. Weekend rolls applied;
   federal legal holidays not yet modelled (dates err early, never late).
   The *sweep* is now real AND nationwide (ADR 0003): `hestia_api.sweep`
   reads portfolio facts (live properties, leases, policies, un-retired debt,
   un-failed exchanges, un-dissolved entities), resolves appeal windows
   through `jurisdiction_chain()` × `jurisdiction_rules` and the per-state
   calendar registry (KY + OH registered; twin-implemented TS/Py), and writes
   anchored `deadlines` rows whose citations and instructions come from pack
   data; the `deadlines_sweep_identity` NULLS-NOT-DISTINCT index makes every
   rerun a database-level no-op, proven by test. Where no pack covers a
   property the sweep emits a typed coverage gap, never a guess — and
   `GET /coverage/jurisdictions` reports known/unknown per rule domain.
   Spent clocks stay silent — a §1031 identification already run does not
   alert. The cross-river acceptance test (Newport KY + Cincinnati OH +

## Review hardening (2026-08-26) — the adversarial pass over increments 2–4

A four-lens adversarial review (money-path correctness, concurrency,
sign/semantics, hostile input) of bank import, reporting, rent, and payments
produced ~20 confirmed defects; every one is fixed and pinned by a named
regression in `services/api/tests/test_review_hardening.py` (schema module
`014_review_hardening.sql` carries the database-level guarantees):

- **Balance fan-out** — lease balances were a JOIN that double-counted when a
  charge had multiple partial allocations; now scalar subqueries, plus a
  persistent `open_credit` (receipts nothing was open for) that
  `apply_open_credit` drains oldest-first on every receipt and sweep — a
  prepayment now auto-pays next month's charge instead of vanishing.
- **Reversal integrity** — reversing a receipt releases its allocations and
  re-opens the charges; a partial UNIQUE index (`one_reversal_per_event`)
  makes the double-reversal race a 409 for the loser; register gross totals
  exclude reversal pairs so churn can't inflate income.
- **Over-allocation is now impossible at the database** — a trigger
  (`refuse_over_allocation`) locks the charge and refuses allocations beyond
  its amount, whatever the application layer does.
- **Check-then-act paths take row locks** — webhook settlement, bank-import
  accept (double-accept is a 409, one ledger event), and the open-charge scan.
- **Schedule E is signed-honest** — a refund in an expense category reports
  as a negative expense line, never `abs()`-forged into income; sign-offs
  store certified totals and the API reports `stale` when the live ledger
  has moved since certification.
- **Payments** — one in-flight request per lease (409), default collect
  amount nets off open credit, provider transport failure rolls back the
  request row entirely (no phantom row; retry works, 502), webhook HMAC over
  raw bytes with multi-`v1` rotation support, non-JSON-but-signed payloads
  rejected as bad signatures, unconfigured secret is a 503 before any parse.
- **Bank import** — BOM-tolerant decode, per-account `invert_amounts` for
  credit-card exports that print charges positive, dispositions guarded by
  `AND disposition = 'pending'`, duplicate nickname/unit label surface as 409.
- **Web** — hold/sell aggregates EVERY lien (blended rate, balance-weighted)
  and names an underwater position instead of crashing; the insurance pill
  floors an inadequate position below 100%; collect double-click guard +
  409/503 as status not error; register filter races resolve latest-wins;
  bank-account quick-create asks which entity owns it; renewal offers date
  by the operator clock, not UTC.

The API suite is 137 tests at 100% line+branch; the web suite 112 at 100%.
Remaining work items surfaced by the same pass were filed as issues #37–#50.
